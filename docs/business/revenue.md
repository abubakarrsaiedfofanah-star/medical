# SAIED revenue model

1. Patient core access can remain free.
2. Professional subscription for advanced provider tools.
3. Pharmacy/clinic/laboratory monthly SaaS subscriptions.
4. Hospital enterprise plans.
5. Transaction/service fees where legally permitted.
6. Home-care booking/service platform fees where permitted.
7. Optional paid integrations and analytics.

Do not sell patient medical data. Revenue should come from transparent services/subscriptions and compliant transaction fees.
